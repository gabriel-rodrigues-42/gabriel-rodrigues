Build your own FSRI portfolio

The FSRI is the fund I propose to bring industry and technology back to Brazil. Here you play investor: pick companies, follow the rules and see what could happen to your money.

The companies are made up. The FSRI does not exist yet. The names, sectors and numbers on each card are only there to show the rules.

The scenarios use what similar Brazilian funds returned from 1994 to 2022. This is not a promise of returns.

The FSRI's advantage is in the tax, not in the return. For people with very high incomes, the investment takes part off income tax. For people who pay little tax, fixed income earns about the same with no risk.

    What this result means

    In the average scenario, the FSRI earns a little more than the CDI. Through the large door, the loan interest eats almost all of that difference, and the income-tax discount may make the math work.

    LCA notes and infrastructure bonds are already tax-exempt and left out of the minimum tax (Law 15,270 of 2025). That's why the FSRI gives the income-tax discount: without it, wealthy people would stay in LCA notes. For people who pay little tax, fixed income is still the right choice.

    How the math works

    The 387 Brazilian funds that buy stakes in companies ended, on average, with 1.1 times what the CDI would pay in the same time. The average scenario does the same with the CDI you choose, for any period. That's a simplification: the study measures each fund's whole life. Between the top and bottom quarters of funds, the gap was 25.3 points a year. The bad and good scenarios sit half that gap below and above the average. The bad one isn't the worst case: the worst is losing everything, shown in "What if every company goes under?".

    The same rate applies to the whole portfolio, because the study does not split funds by the FSRI tiers. If the rules are not met, the profit pays 15% tax, like a bank CD after two years. With the rules met, the profit is tax-free and, through the large door, the investment takes 2% of its value a year off income tax, for 5 years, up to half of that year's tax.

    Through the large door, loan interest grows with the CDI plus the margin you enter, and comes out of every line. LCA notes assume 85% of the CDI. With rates "Falling", each year's CDI follows the Selic rate projected in the Focus survey minus 0.10 point, and stays at 10.4% after 2029. FSRI company returns keep today's CDI, so the difference shows only the effect of lower rates. Stocks and real-estate funds repeat the Ibovespa (+35.4%) and the IFIX (+31.5%) from 2021 to 2025.

    Insurance only pays if the company really goes bankrupt, confirmed by an audit. It does not cover profit that never came.
