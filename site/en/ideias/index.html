Does the voice sound robotic? You can install a better one for free
  • Chrome (any computer): pick the Google US or Google UK voice in the caption bar. It needs internet.
  • Edge (Windows or Mac): pick Aria (natural), Jenny (natural) or Guy (natural). They are the most natural English voices.
  • Mac and iPhone (Safari): open System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › System Voice › Manage Voices, look for English and download a Premium or Enhanced voice, such as Ava or Zoe. Then reload this page.
  • Android: in Settings › Text-to-speech output, use Google's engine and download the English voice.
