No names

Some people bring up politics just to find out which side you're on. These comebacks use characters from films, cartoons and Brazilian TV, so you can answer without naming anyone. Pick what they said and get a comeback.

How to use it without starting a fight

  • Mock the way of doing politics, not the person.
  • Don't accuse anyone of a crime, not even as a joke. A joke that accuses can end up in court.
  • The characters fit politicians from any party. Listeners can decide who they're thinking of.
  • After the joke, steer to a real problem: taxes, queues, paperwork.
