Humor with no side

No names

Some people start a political chat only to find out which side you're on. Here are comebacks with characters from films, cartoons and soap operas, so you can answer without naming anyone. Pick what they said to you and draw a way out.

How to use it without starting a fight

  • Mock the way of doing politics, not the person.
  • Don't accuse anyone of a crime, not even as a joke. A joke that accuses can end up in court.
  • The characters fit politicians from any party. Whoever hears it links them to whoever they like.
  • After the joke, steer to a real problem: taxes, queues, paperwork.
