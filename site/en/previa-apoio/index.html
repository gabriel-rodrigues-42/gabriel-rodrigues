Support a proposal with one tap

Three layouts for the thumbs up on every proposal. Open each one on a real page and tap it. Nothing is saved in the preview and the numbers are examples.

  1. A. Thumbs up at the top

    A pill button right after the opening, before the text. Easy to see, but the person supports before reading.

    See on Reindustrialization or Cannabis

  2. B. Card at the end (recommended)

    A card before "Help improve this proposal", with the question "Does this proposal make sense to you?". The person supports after reading.

    See on Reindustrialization or Cannabis

  3. C. Button that follows the reading

    A round button in the corner that shows up once the title leaves the screen and hides near the footer. Always at hand, but it sits over the text.

    See on Reindustrialization or Cannabis

After supporting

In all three, a thank-you note shows up with Share (on the phone), WhatsApp and Copy link. The number only shows from 10 supports.
